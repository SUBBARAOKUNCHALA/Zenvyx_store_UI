Privacy Policy

Zenvyx Store

At Zenvyx Store, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use and protect that information, when we may share it, and the choices available to you when you use our website, create an account, purchase products, or contact us.

By using the Zenvyx Store website or services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Zenvyx Store, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and other information required to create and manage your account.
  • Login Information: Information associated with authentication methods you use, including supported third-party login services such as Google Sign-In.
  • Order Information: Products purchased, order details, order status, transaction references, delivery information, and related order history.
  • Address Information: Billing and/or shipping address details that you provide when placing an order.
  • Payment Information: Payment-related information necessary to process your transaction. Online payments are processed through authorized third-party payment gateway providers. We do not intend to store your complete card credentials on our own servers.
  • Support Information: Information you provide when contacting us, raising a support ticket, submitting an enquiry, or communicating with our customer support team.
  • Attachments: Files or images you voluntarily submit as part of a support request, such as photographs of damaged or incorrect products.
  • Technical Information: Information such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, pages visited, and other technical information that may be automatically collected when you use our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your Zenvyx Store account.
  • Processing and fulfilling your orders.
  • Processing payments and verifying transactions.
  • Arranging shipping and delivery of products.
  • Providing order confirmations, shipping updates, and other service-related communications.
  • Responding to customer enquiries and support requests.
  • Managing returns, exchanges, refunds, cancellations, and related requests.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, abuse, unauthorized activity, or security incidents.
  • Maintaining, improving, and securing our website, applications, products, and services.
  • Complying with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, tax, and other lawful obligations.

3. Account and Authentication

If you create an account on Zenvyx Store, you are responsible for providing accurate information and maintaining the security of your account credentials.

Where third-party authentication such as Google Sign-In is available, authentication may be handled by the relevant third-party provider. Information received through such authentication is used to create or manage your Zenvyx Store account and provide the requested services.

4. Payments and Financial Information

Zenvyx Store uses third-party payment processing services to facilitate online transactions.

  • Payment transactions may be processed through authorized payment gateway providers, including Razorpay where applicable.
  • Payment providers may collect and process payment information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.
  • Zenvyx Store does not intend to store complete card numbers, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, passwords, or other sensitive payment authentication credentials on its own servers.
  • We may retain transaction-related information such as payment status, order amount, payment reference, and transaction identifiers for order management, customer support, accounting, fraud prevention, and legal purposes.

5. Shipping and Delivery Information

To deliver your purchases, we may share necessary delivery information with shipping and courier service providers.

The information shared may include your name, phone number, shipping address, order details, and other information necessary to complete delivery.

6. Customer Support and Attachments

When you contact Zenvyx Store or create a support ticket, we may collect information necessary to investigate and resolve your request.

If you voluntarily upload photographs, documents, or other attachments as part of a support request, those files may be stored and processed using third-party services that support our customer service operations.

Please avoid uploading sensitive personal information that is not necessary to resolve your support request.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Zenvyx Store may use cookies, local storage, session technologies, and similar technologies to provide essential website functionality and improve the user experience.

These technologies may be used for purposes such as maintaining login sessions, remembering preferences, protecting accounts, maintaining shopping functionality, and understanding website usage where applicable.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information merely for the purpose of selling or renting customer data.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate Zenvyx Store and provide requested services.

Such parties may include:

  • Payment gateway and transaction processing providers.
  • Shipping and courier service providers.
  • Cloud storage, image hosting, or file processing providers used by our website.
  • Authentication and technology service providers.
  • Professional, legal, regulatory, or governmental authorities where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure. Therefore, while we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining transaction records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, enforcing agreements, and complying with applicable legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

The specific retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the reason for which it was collected.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable limitations.
  • Raise questions or concerns regarding the handling of your personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact details provided at the end of this Privacy Policy.

12. Children's Privacy

We may update or modify this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.
